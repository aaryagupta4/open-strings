01 · How it works

The microphone feeds a WebAudio analyser producing 2048-sample frames at the device sample rate. Pitch is estimated via the YIN algorithm — a time-domain method that computes a normalized difference function and picks the first minimum below a fixed threshold, refined by parabolic interpolation. A ±5-sample median smoothes octave errors.

02 · Why fifths

Real violinists tune by ear, string against string, listening for beats to vanish as the interval approaches a pure 3:2. Every browser tuner I could find ignores this. Perfect-fifth mode is the reason this exists.

03 · Roadmap

Real bowed violin samples replace the synthesized reference tones; temperament switcher (A = 440 / 442 / 415 Hz); a vibrato analyzer reporting speed and peak-to-peak depth.
