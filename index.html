How it works

The microphone feeds a WebAudio graph in your browser. Pitch is detected using the YIN algorithm—a time-domain method that estimates the fundamental frequency by finding the point of minimum ‘difference’ in the signal against itself. In fifths mode, a short FFT picks the two strongest low-frequency peaks and reports the beat frequency between their nearest harmonic pair.

Why fifths

Violinists tune by ear, string against string, listening for beats to disappear—not by chasing four separate needles. Every browser tuner I could find ignored this. Perfect-fifth mode is the reason this one exists.
